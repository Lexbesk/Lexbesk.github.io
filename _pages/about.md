I'm an S.M. student in [Computational Science and Engineering](https://seas.harvard.edu/computational-science-and-engineering) at [Harvard University](https://www.harvard.edu/) (GSAS / SEAS). I hold a B.S. in Statistics from the [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.math.pku.edu.cn/puremath_en/) at [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/).

My interests include: Robot Learning, Generative AI, 3D Vision for Robotics, and Computer-Use Agents.

I like building the pieces that let a system be measured honestly — benchmarks, calibration, simulation — and then the models that have to pass those tests. Recently that has meant converting real robot video into physics-consistent simulation, generative models of 3D object-particle dynamics, and long-horizon office workflows for GUI agents.

Most recently I was a research intern in the Qingyun Program at [Tencent](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/), where I was a core contributor to [UI-Mate](https://github.com/Tencent/UI-Mate), the Hy Team's foundation GUI agent, and owned task construction for its **OSWorkerBench** benchmark.

Before that, I was a research associate with Dr. [Katerina Fragkiadaki](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~katef/) in the [Machine Learning Department](https://www.ml.cmu.edu/) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), and a research assistant at the [OV<sup>3</sup> Lab](https://zhoujiahuan1991.github.io/research.html) under Dr. [Jiahuan Zhou](https://zhoujiahuan1991.github.io/) in the [Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology](https://www.icst.pku.edu.cn/english/home/index.htm), [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/).

Away from the screen you'll usually find me on a tennis or pickleball court, at a piano, or over a chessboard.
