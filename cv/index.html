    Beijing, China

    Research Intern, Qingyun Program
    Tencent
    Core contributor, UI-Mate technical report — the foundation GUI agent from Tencent's Hy Team.
    • Owned task construction for OSWorkerBench, a benchmark of 100 long-horizon office workflows spanning 41 applications and 10 job families, released with UI-Mate.
    • Designed the capability-grounded task generation pipeline and made every task automatically scoreable by weighted checkpoints on final application state, then refined them through iterative human-in-the-loop verification using pilot-agent rollouts and synthetic test cases.

Projects

  • 2026.01 - 2026.05
    Multi-Skill Dexterous Manipulation: RL to flow-matching
    Advised by Dr. He Wang.
    • Built a three-stage learning pipeline for dexterous grasping: a flow-matching pose proposer conditioned on object point clouds, an RL pick-up policy rewarded against proposed grasps to prevent mode collapse, and a visuomotor student distilled from RL demonstrations.
    • Injected noise during demonstration collection to collect recovery behaviors from the teacher, addressing behavioral-cloning distribution shift; reached 85%+ success across Dexonomy with diverse grasp strategies.
